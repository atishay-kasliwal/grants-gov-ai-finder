Grants.gov Search

Find recent AI, data, and software-related grant opportunities.

This minimal frontend searches the live Grants.gov API through the local wrapper. It treats "recent" as opportunities with an openDate in the last N days.

Funding Dashboards

Best U.S. grant portals for this search

These links complement the Grants.gov search form with startup, research, and healthcare-focused funding sources.

Federal Core

Grants.gov

The main portal for nearly all federal grant opportunities and the source behind this dashboard’s live search.

  • Best for broad federal discovery
  • Good starting point for AI, software, and commercialization terms
Open Grants.gov

Cleaner Search

Simpler Grants

A cleaner interface for browsing active federal opportunities when you want a faster visual scan than the legacy portal.

  • Best for browsing open opportunities
  • Useful as a second opinion on search phrasing
Open Simpler Grants

Startup Funding

SBIR.gov

Strong fit for technology startups, research products, AI, healthcare tools, and innovation funding programs.

  • Best for commercialization-oriented grants
  • Especially relevant for early product teams
Open SBIR.gov

Seed Capital

NSF America’s Seed Fund

Funding for early-stage technology companies, with strong relevance for research commercialization and software innovation.

  • Best for deep-tech startups
  • Good match for AI product ideas and IP-heavy work
Open NSF Seed Fund

Health + Biomed

NIH Grants

Best for healthcare, medical AI, biomedical research, and clinical or translational project funding.

  • Best for medical AI and research teams
  • Relevant when the product touches clinical workflows or biomedical data
Open NIH Grants

Start Here

Best first stops for a patent and AI product idea

Begin with Grants.gov, NSF America’s Seed Fund, and SBIR.gov. Those three give you the best spread across federal discovery, startup-oriented R&D funding, and commercialization pathways.
